The Problem We Solved

The Illusion of the Percentage Score

In standard exams, two learners with identical 50% scores are treated identically. But their cognitive flaws are completely divergent:

Learner A • Priya

Score: 50% (3 / 6)

Impetus Fallacy

Believes moving objects store internal force that gets consumed. High confidence (5/5) in wrong answers. Needs: Counter-example cognitive conflict to break the flawed mental model.

Diagnosis: Overconfidence Trap in Newton's 1st Law (p=0.88)
Learner B • Aarav

Score: 50% (3 / 6)

Action-Reaction Confusion

Thinks Newton's 3rd law pairs cancel each other out on the same object. Low confidence (2/5) with hesitation. Needs: Free-body diagram boundary separation and vector scaffolding.

Diagnosis: Third-Law Pair Cancellation (p=0.91)

Giving both students the same remedial worksheet guarantees failure. Misconception Mapper creates completely individualized diagnostic and remediation trajectories.

Engineering Innovations

Built for Rigorous Diagnostic Accuracy

Designed from the ground up without LLM hallucinations on grading or arbitrary classifications.

01

Longitudinal Loss Attribution

Classifies errors into 4 canonical categories: Conceptual, Overconfidence Traps, Formula Confusion, and Slips across all student history.

02

Prescriptive Learning Path

Ranks gaps by downstream concepts blocked and revision priority. Directly spotlights Rank #1 targets on the interactive knowledge graph.

03

Multimodal RAG with Figure Citations

Extracts diagrams and equations from textbooks using Tesseract OCR with LLM caption fallback, citing exact pages and figures in explanations.

04

5-Node Progress Journey

Real-time per-misconception lifecycle: Diagnosed → Learned → Practiced → Verified → Mastered, computed dynamically without duplicated storage.

05

Calibration Trajectory

Longitudinal calibration tracking measuring whether high confidence aligns with actual correctness, preventing dangerous overconfidence traps.

06

Teacher Cohort Heatmap

Provides classroom-wide metacognitive matrices, student drilldown profiles, and automated lesson-plan focus recommendations for educators.
